Threat hunting usually assumes you can call out to a large model. In an **air-gapped** environment you cannot: nothing leaves the network, and whatever runs has to fit on the hardware already in the room.

RINA Expert fine-tunes **Small Language Models** for threat-hunting tasks so that the whole thing executes locally — lightweight enough to be practical, contained enough to be secure.

Awarded **Best Innovation Idea of the Year** at Viettel Cyber Security (2026).
